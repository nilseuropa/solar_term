### Parts

- 1x **Waveshare ESP32-S3-RLCD-4.2** board _(not a similarly named ESP32-S3 or e-paper board)_
- 1x SD Card _(optional, but recommended)_
- 1x Rii 518BT Mini BLE keyboard
- 1x 18650 lithium battery
- 4x original screws from the Waveshare KIT
- 4x M3 x 5 mm screws
- 4x M3 inserts
- (optional) [Plexiglass](stl/plexiglass.dxf) 2mm cutout
- [Bezel](../stl/ata/Bezel.stl)
- [Case back](../stl/poc_top.stl)
- [Button covers](../stl/poc_button.stl)

### Assembly

#### 1. Print all components
![](ata/image_(9).png)

#### 2. Put the M3 inserts into Case back
_( Make sure they are all flush. )_
![](ata/image_(8).png) ![](ata/image_(7).png)

#### 3. Glue in the self adhesive speaker and place the Button covers
![](ata/image_(6).png)

#### 4. Connect the speaker and insert the display.
_( Make sure not to pinch the wires. )_
![](ata/image_(5).png)

#### 5. Use the 4 original screws to mount display into the Case back
![](ata/image_(4).png)

#### 6. Mount the Bezel to the Case back
_Use the 4 x m3x5mm screws to mount the bezel onto the caseback. If you have a protective plexiglass sheet, insert it before you screw down the bezel. You could insert the keyboard as well, but it should be able to slip in afterwards too._
![](ata/image_(3).png)

#### 7. Insert the battery and install SolarOS.
![](ata/image_(2).png)

#### 8. Enjoy
![](ata/image_(1).png)
